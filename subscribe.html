Frequently Asked Questions

When do picks drop each day?
Picks are typically published by 11 AM ET each day, before the first games tip off. Premium subscribers get an email alert the moment they're live.
What sports do you cover?
MLB, NHL (including playoffs), and NBA (when in season). We pick from whichever sports have games that day for the best props available.
How is the record tracked?
Every pick is logged in our Daily Recaps archive with a ✓ (Win) or ✗ (Loss) once results are final. No cherry-picking — the record reflects every tracked pick, including losses.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — cancel at any time with no penalty. Your subscription stays active until the end of your billing period.
Are these guaranteed wins?
No. Sports betting always involves risk. InsideProps uses data and analysis to find edges, but no pick is ever guaranteed. Bet responsibly and only what you can afford to lose.
What's the Parlay of the Day?
Every day, Premium subscribers get a 4–6 leg prop parlay targeting minimum +2500 odds. These are high-risk, high-reward slips built from the same data that drives our top picks.
